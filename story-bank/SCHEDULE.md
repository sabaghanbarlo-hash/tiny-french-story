# Tiny French - posting calendar (Nov 7 - Dec 6, 2026)

Every script posts at 14:00 UTC (17:30 Tehran, 15:00 Paris) on the date in its file name. The daily refill workflow renders each one about 4 days earlier and puts it in the posting queue.
To move a Reel to another day, rename its two files (`.txt` and `.caption`) with the new date. To add a Reel, drop in `YYYYMMDD1400-name.txt` + `.caption`. To skip one, delete both files.
