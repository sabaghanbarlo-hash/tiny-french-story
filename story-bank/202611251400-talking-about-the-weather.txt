title: Talking About the Weather
cast: Alia, Nino
episode: 32
scene: park

Nino: Salut Alia ! Quel temps fait-il aujourd'hui ? | Hi Alia! What's the weather like today?
Alia: Il fait froid et il y a du vent. | It's cold and it's windy.
Nino: Il pleut aussi ? | Is it raining too?
Alia: Non, mais il y a des nuages. | No, but there are clouds.
Nino: Et demain ? | And tomorrow?
Alia: Demain, il va neiger ! | Tomorrow, it's going to snow!
Nino: Super ! J'adore la neige ! | Great! I love snow!
Alia: Moi, je préfère le soleil ! | I prefer the sun!
