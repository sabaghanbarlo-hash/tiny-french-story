title: Never say actually like this in French
episode: 36
scene: cafe

Adrien: Actuellement, je n'aime pas le café ! | Actually, I don't like coffee! (it means "currently"!)
Marinette: Non, non ! | No, no!
Marinette: En fait, je n'aime pas le café ! | Actually, I don't like coffee!

vocab: En fait | on FET | Actually / in fact
