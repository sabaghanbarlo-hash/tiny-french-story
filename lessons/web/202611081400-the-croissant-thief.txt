title: The Croissant Thief
cast: Bugaboo, Cat
episode: 15
scene: street
cover: Croissant Thief

Bugaboo: Regarde ! Quelqu'un court avec des croissants ! | Look! Someone is running with croissants!
Cat: C'est un voleur ! Il court vite ! | It's a thief! He runs fast!
Bugaboo: Vite, attrape-le ! | Quick, catch him!
Cat: Je l'ai ! Rends les croissants ! | I've got him! Give back the croissants!
Bugaboo: Pourquoi tu les voles ? | Why are you stealing them?
Cat: Il dit qu'il a très faim. | He says he's very hungry.
Bugaboo: Pauvre voleur ! Tiens, un croissant. | Poor thief! Here, a croissant.
Cat: Bravo, Bugaboo ! Tout le monde est content ! | Well done, Bugaboo! Everyone is happy!

vocab: un voleur | a thief
vocab: attraper | to catch
vocab: j'ai faim | I'm hungry
vocab: pauvre | poor
vocab: rendre | to give back
