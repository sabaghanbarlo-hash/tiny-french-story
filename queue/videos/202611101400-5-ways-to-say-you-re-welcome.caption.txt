5 ways French people actually say "you're welcome" 🙏🇫🇷

• je t'en prie = you're welcome
• c'est gentil = that's kind
• de rien = it's nothing
• il n'y a pas de quoi = don't mention it
• merci mille fois = a thousand thanks
