Taking a taxi in Paris? Say THIS 🚕🇫🇷

• où allez-vous ? = where are you going?
• à vingt minutes = twenty minutes away
• environ = roughly
• je peux payer par carte ? = can I pay by card?
• on y va = let's go

💬 Where would you go first in Paris? Comment "À la Tour Eiffel !" or your spot 👇
